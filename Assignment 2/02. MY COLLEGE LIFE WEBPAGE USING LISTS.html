5. Description List – College Terminology

Semester
A division of the academic year into a period of study.
Lecture
A teaching session conducted by a teacher or professor.
Assignment
A task given to students to complete as part of their studies.
Attendance
The record of a student's presence in classes.
Examination
A test used to evaluate a student's knowledge and understanding.
